About HMS Hospital & Medical Centre

HMS Hospital & Medical Centre is a state-of-the-art multi-specialty clinical facility dedicated to delivering compassionate, evidence-based healthcare. Our facility houses 500+ beds, 12 advanced modular operation theaters, and 24/7 emergency response.

  • JCI and NABH Certified Excellence
  • Comprehensive Diagnostic & Imaging Center
  • 24/7 Level 1 Trauma Care Center
